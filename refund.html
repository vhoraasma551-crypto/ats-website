Cancellation & Refund Policy

1. When you pay

In ATS, payment for a service is made after the work, on a bill the customer has seen. So cancelling a booking before the work starts normally means there is nothing to pay and nothing to refund.

2. Cancelling a booking

  • A customer can cancel a booking in the app while it is "Requested" or "Accepted", free of charge. Please cancel as early as possible, and do not make repeated bookings you do not intend to keep; repeated no-shows or last-minute cancellations may lead to restrictions.
  • A provider can reject a request. Providers should not cancel accepted bookings without a good reason; repeated cancellations may lead to restrictions.
  • If the provider does not arrive or the service is not done, tell us through a complaint in the app.

3. If you do not agree with the bill

The customer can choose "I do not agree" on the bill. The provider and customer should first try to agree through chat. If they cannot, the customer or provider can send a complaint with the booking ID. ATS will review the booking, the chat, any before/after photos and the complaint, and may ask both sides for more information. ATS decides in good faith and its decision is final for the purposes of the ATS platform, without affecting your legal rights.

4. Refunds for online payments

If ATS finds that a refund is due (for example the service was not done, you were charged twice, or the amount is wrong), we will refund the amount to the original payment method through Razorpay. Refunds are normally started within 7 working days of our decision. The time for the money to reach your account depends on your bank or payment provider and is usually a further 5 to 7 working days. The provider's earnings for that booking may be reduced or reversed.

5. Cash payments

For cash payments the money was paid directly to the provider, so ATS cannot return cash. ATS can review the complaint and take action against the provider, but the refund must be settled with the provider.

6. Membership fees

Membership fees are not refundable once the membership is active. We will refund duplicate payments, or payments where the membership was not activated, or where the law requires it.

7. Withdrawal failures

If a withdrawal to a provider's bank fails or is rejected, the amount is returned to the provider's ATS Pocket.

8A. Shopping goods (Video Call shopping)

  • Goods approved by the customer on the video call are not refundable because of a change of mind.
  • If the goods are wrong, damaged or not what was shown and approved, the customer should tell the provider in chat and send a complaint with photos within 24 hours of delivery. The provider should try to exchange or return the goods to the shop where the shop allows it.
  • If the shop returns the money, the goods amount is returned to the customer. For online payments ATS can refund through Razorpay after review; for cash the refund is settled between customer and provider. The service charge is not refunded if the shopping and delivery were done as booked.
  • ATS reviews each complaint and may decide on a case-by-case basis. We are not able to guarantee a shop's return policy.

8. How to ask for a refund or cancellation help

Open the booking in the app and tap "Complaint", or write to vhoramahmadmubin7@gmail.com with your booking ID. Include what went wrong and any photos. We aim to reply within 3 working days.
