ATS - All Service of All Time

ATS is a mobile app that connects customers with independent local service providers in India, such as electricians, plumbers, tutors, cleaners and many more services. Customers search for a service, book a provider and pay securely online through Razorpay.

How it works

  • Customers create an account, search a service and book a provider.
  • Payment for the service is made securely online (UPI, cards, netbanking) through Razorpay.
  • Providers receive their earnings in their ATS wallet and can withdraw to their bank account.
  • ATS charges a platform commission of 7% on each completed booking.

Optional membership

Rs 49 for 30 days. Customers get a 2% discount and providers earn 2% extra during the membership.
