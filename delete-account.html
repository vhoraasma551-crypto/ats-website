Delete Your ATS Account

Delete inside the app

Open the ATS app, go to Profile > Settings > Delete account and confirm. Your login, profile and bank details are deleted.

If you cannot open the app

Write to vhoramahmadmubin7@gmail.com from the email address you used to register, with the subject "Delete my ATS account". We will confirm your identity and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted and what we keep

  • Deleted: your login, profile, saved addresses, emergency contact and bank details.
  • Kept for the period needed by law and for dispute handling: records of bookings, payments, commission and withdrawals, complaints and safety alerts, and other information we must keep under Indian law.
  • Uploaded photos and documents that are not removed automatically are deleted on request.

Before deleting, withdraw any ATS Pocket balance, because a deleted account cannot receive payouts.
