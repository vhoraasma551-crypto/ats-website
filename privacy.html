Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

ATS - All Service of All Time ("ATS", "we", "us") is a mobile app that connects customers with independent service providers. ATS is operated by ATS - Mahamadmuubin Mahebubhai Vahora (Sole Proprietor), Vansol, Anand, Gujarat, India, India.

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using ATS you agree to this policy. Last updated: 23 November 2026.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, city, address and saved addresses, and your password (managed securely by Firebase Authentication; we cannot read it).
  • Gender: chosen by you at sign-up (female, male or other), used only to offer and enforce women-only services such as Girl Taxi.
  • Your role and activity: whether you are a customer or provider, bookings, chat messages, ratings and reviews, complaints, and notifications.
  • Location: your approximate or precise location when you allow it, to show nearby providers and fill your address. If you are a provider and switch on "Share my live location" for a booking, your location is sent to ATS and shown to that booking's customer while the app is open.
  • Photos and files: profile and work photos, before/after photos of a job, offer photos, and for providers identity, certificate and address-proof documents and a bank passbook photo.
  • Bank details (providers only): account holder name, account number and IFSC, used only to pay out your earnings.
  • Emergency contact (optional): the name and phone number of a person you choose, used only when you press the SOS options.
  • Safety alerts: if you send an SOS alert, we receive your name, phone, booking details and (if available) your location.
  • Payment details: payments are handled by Razorpay. We do not receive or store your card, UPI or bank login details. We store payment IDs, order IDs, amounts and status.
  • AI Helper text: if you use the AI Helper, the problem text and budget you type are sent to our server and to an AI service (Anthropic) to suggest a service. Do not type sensitive personal information there.
  • Shopping bills and video calls: for shopping services the provider's bill lists the items bought and their prices, and may include a photo of the shop bill. ATS does not record or store video calls; they happen through your phone or video-call app, which has its own privacy rules. A provider's profile photo is shown as chosen by the provider (to everyone, or only after a booking is confirmed).
  • Location shown to others: a profile's location is shown to other users only approximately (about 1 km). The exact location pin is shared only with the other person of a booking, as described above, and with ATS admins.
  • Review photos: if you add a photo to a review, it is visible to the provider of that booking, to you and to ATS admins.
  • Login with Google: if you choose Login with Google, Google tells us your name and email address (we never see your Google password). We use them only to create and secure your ATS account.
  • Device information: a notification token so we can send you push notifications, and basic technical logs created by our cloud providers.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account, show providers and services, take and manage bookings, and let you chat and rate.
  • To calculate bills, commission, discounts and membership benefits, process payments and pay out provider earnings.
  • To verify providers and keep customers and providers safe, handle complaints and SOS alerts, and prevent fraud and misuse.
  • To send service messages and notifications, and, only if you agreed at sign-up, offers and updates about ATS.
  • To improve the app, fix problems and follow the law.

4. Who can see your information

  • Customer and provider for a booking: the provider sees the customer's name, phone number, address, map location pin and problem for that booking (before accepting, only the approximate distance is shown in the app); the customer sees the provider's name, phone number, city, rating, reviews, timings, work photos and (when shared) live location.
  • Other users: a provider's public profile (name, city, services, rates, rating, work photos, timings) is visible to customers. Reviews you write show your booking's service and your rating.
  • ATS team: our administrators can see account, booking, complaint, SOS, document and bank information as needed to run the service, verify providers, pay out earnings and resolve problems.
  • Service providers we use: Google Firebase (login, database, file storage, notifications, server functions), Razorpay and RazorpayX (payments and payouts), Anthropic (AI Helper), OpenStreetMap (map tiles; your device requests map images from their servers), and Google Fonts (to show Hindi/Gujarati names in receipts). These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms.
  • Legal and safety: we may share information if required by law, court order or a government authority, or to protect the rights, safety and property of users and ATS.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Location

We ask for location permission only while the app is in use. You can refuse or turn it off in your phone settings; some features (nearby providers, map, automatic address) will then work less well. Live location sharing by providers is optional per booking, stops when the booking ends or when the provider switches it off, and works only while the app is open.

6. Photos and documents

Identity documents, certificates, address proof and passbook photos are stored in private storage. They can be opened only by you and ATS administrators. Before/after job photos can be opened by the customer, the provider and ATS administrators of that booking. A provider's profile photo is shown to other users as the provider has chosen (to everyone, or only after a booking is confirmed; some services require it to be shown). A copy of the profile photo is also kept in your private contact details. Review photos can be opened only by the customer, the provider of that booking and ATS administrators. You can change or remove your photos in the app, except where a photo is required for a service you offer.

7. Notifications and marketing

We send notifications about bookings, payments, chat messages, complaints and safety. You can turn notifications off in your phone settings. Marketing messages are sent only if you ticked the consent box at sign-up; you can ask us to stop at any time.

8. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account in the app (Profile > Settings > Delete account), we delete your login, profile and bank details. We may keep records of bookings, payments, commission, complaints and safety alerts, and other information we are required to keep, for the period needed for accounting, tax, dispute handling, fraud prevention and legal obligations under Indian law. Uploaded files that are not deleted automatically are removed on request. To request deletion or removal without the app, see the Delete Account page or write to vhoramahmadmubin7@gmail.com.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including access rules in our database and storage, encrypted connections, and server-side checks for payments. No system is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. Keep your password private and log out on shared devices.

10. Your rights and choices

You may ask us to: give you a copy of your information, correct wrong information (you can also edit most of it in the app), delete your account and information, withdraw your consent (for example for marketing or location), and respond to a complaint about how your information is handled. Write to vhoramahmadmubin7@gmail.com. We may need to confirm your identity first.

11. Children

ATS is for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version is always in the app (Help > Privacy Policy) and on our website. For important changes we will tell you in the app.
